ChannelDeck

A private desktop app for running a small network of YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Shows each connected channel's subscribers, views and per-video numbers, and how they change over time.
  • Lists new comments and lets the channel owner reply to them after reviewing a suggested reply.
  • Keeps a production guide for each channel so new videos follow the same process.

Who it is for

ChannelDeck is used by its developer and family to manage their own channels. It is not offered as a public service and has no sign-up.

Google sign-in

The app signs in with Google so it can read the owner's channel data through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and post comment replies the owner approves. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and where it is stored.
