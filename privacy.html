Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What ChannelDeck accesses

When you sign in with Google and choose a YouTube channel, ChannelDeck requests:

  • youtube.force-ssl: to read the channel, its videos and comments, and to post replies to comments when you press Send.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read view, watch-time and subscriber statistics.

ChannelDeck never deletes videos or comments and never changes channel settings.

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok

If you connect them, ChannelDeck reads your Facebook Pages (name, followers, posts with their reactions and comments), your Instagram professional account (profile, followers, posts with likes, comments and views) or your TikTok account (profile, followers, likes, videos with their views, likes, comments and shares). It never posts, deletes or changes anything on these platforms.

Where your data is stored

  • Everything stays on your own computer. ChannelDeck has no server and no account system.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager on your computer.
  • Channel statistics and comments are cached in a local database on your computer so growth can be shown over time.

Sharing

ChannelDeck does not sell, share or transfer your Google or YouTube data to anyone. It is not used for advertising.

When you ask ChannelDeck to suggest a reply to a comment, the comment text is processed by the AI assistant installed on your own computer (Claude Code) to draft the reply. Nothing is posted until you review it and press Send.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ChannelDeck's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ChannelDeck also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can disconnect a channel inside ChannelDeck, which deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the ChannelDeck folder in your AppData removes all locally stored data.
